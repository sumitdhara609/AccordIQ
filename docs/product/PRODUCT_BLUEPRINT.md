# AccordIQ Product Blueprint

## Product

**Name:** AccordIQ

**Tagline:** Turn documents into trusted data.

---

## Vision

AccordIQ is an intelligent document understanding platform that transforms unstructured documents into trusted structured data using OCR, AI, and validation.

---

## Problem Statement

Businesses spend significant time extracting, validating, and organizing information from invoices, receipts, forms, and other documents. Existing OCR tools often produce inaccurate or incomplete results that require manual correction.

AccordIQ solves this by combining OCR, intelligent extraction, validation, confidence scoring, and human review into a single workflow.
